About the Shelter "Cozy House"

Each dog is given a cozy space, nutritious meals, and medical attention to ensure they feel safe and healthy.

Volunteers and caregivers spend time playing, training, and bonding with the dogs, helping them rediscover trust and joy.

Our mission is to prepare these dogs for adoption, connecting them with families who will cherish them forever.
